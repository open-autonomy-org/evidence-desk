### Purpose

To establish clear guidelines and requirements for employee behavior and responsibilities in regard to security, confidentiality, and integrity.

### Scope

This policy covers all {{organization}} employees including interns, contractors, part-time and full-time staff.

### Policy

#### Reference Checks
As part of its hiring process, {{organization}} does not perform criminal background checks, but does employ a reference check process for full time, prospective employment candidates prior to or within 30 days of their hire date.

#### Security Awareness training

All employees must complete {{organization}}’s information security awareness training as part of their initial onboarding and thereafter, while still under contract, on an annual basis.

#### Performance Reviews

All full time employees must complete an annual Performance Review, the results of which are signed and dated by both the employee and their manager, and uploaded to the employee’s personnel files in the HR system.

### Roles and responsibilities

{{organization}}’s People Operations team is responsible for conducting reference checks and managing the performance review process. The Security Team is responsible for security awareness training assignments.
