# Acceptable use policy

## Purpose

This policy sets the rules for using {{organization}}'s systems, accounts and the devices that reach them.

## Scope

It applies to everyone with access to {{organization}}'s systems, on any device used for that access.

## Accounts

- Use only your own accounts. Never share passwords, tokens or multi-factor devices.
- Turn on multi-factor authentication wherever it is offered.
- Keep credentials in the approved password manager or secret store, never in source code, chat or documents.

## Devices

Devices used to reach {{organization}}'s systems must:

- encrypt their disks;
- lock the screen automatically after a short period of inactivity;
- install operating system and browser updates promptly;
- run the malware protection the operating system provides or {{organization}} specifies.

Report a lost or stolen device immediately, as the incident response policy describes.

## Data

- Handle information according to its classification.
- Do not copy customer data to personal accounts, personal devices or unapproved services.
- Do not give customer data, confidential source code or secrets to an outside service, including an AI tool, unless that service is approved for that class of data.

## Monitoring

{{organization}} may monitor its systems for security purposes, as the logging and monitoring policy describes.

## Violations

A violation may lead to removal of access and disciplinary action.
