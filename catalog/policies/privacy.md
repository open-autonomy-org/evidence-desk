## Purpose

This policy sets how {{organization}} collects, uses, keeps, discloses and disposes of personal information, and how it honors the commitments in its privacy notice.

## Notice and choice

- {{organization}} publishes a privacy notice that states what personal information it collects, why, who it is shared with and how individuals exercise their rights.
- Individuals are offered the choices the notice describes, and consent is obtained and recorded where required.

## Collection, use and retention

- Personal information is collected only for the purposes in the notice and used only for them.
- It is kept only as long as the retention schedule allows, then disposed of securely.
- It is kept accurate and complete for its purpose.

## Individual rights

Requests to access, correct or delete personal information are recorded and answered within the time the notice commits to.

## Disclosure

- Personal information is disclosed to third parties only as the notice allows, and each disclosure is recorded.
- Third parties that receive personal information are bound by agreement to protect it and to report unauthorized disclosure.
- Breaches are notified to affected individuals and authorities as law and commitments require, under the incident response policy.

## Inquiries and complaints

Privacy inquiries and complaints are recorded, answered and reviewed for recurring issues.
