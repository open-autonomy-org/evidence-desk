# Code of conduct

## Purpose

{{organization}} expects everyone who works for it or on its behalf to act with integrity, to treat people with respect and to protect the information customers trust it with.

## Scope

This code applies to employees, contractors and anyone else acting for {{organization}}.

## Standards

- Act honestly. Do not misrepresent facts to customers, colleagues, auditors or regulators.
- Protect confidential information, including customer data, as the data classification policy requires.
- Avoid conflicts of interest, and disclose any that arise to the security owner or management.
- Treat people with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.
- Follow the law and {{organization}}'s policies. When a policy and a request conflict, ask before acting.
- Report suspected violations of this code or of any policy through the channel named in the incident response policy. Reports made in good faith are never grounds for retaliation.

## Acknowledgment

Each person acknowledges this code when they join and each year after.

## Violations

A violation may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including ending the working relationship.
